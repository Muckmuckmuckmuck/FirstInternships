# Directory release and monetization

## What is implemented

The build prerenders 135 React routes and includes two maintained legal pages. Search engines and visitors without JavaScript can read the program/application-guide content and follow ordinary links. Saved, comparison, and 404 pages are noindex; 134 maintained URLs are included in the sitemap. Query-string searches and program-preselection links canonicalize to their underlying page.

Eighty-nine official-source program pathways now span technology, engineering, finance, business, biomedical and scientific research, museums, media, retail, entertainment, consulting, aerospace, logistics, hospitality, manufacturing, energy, government, and public service. The expanded inventory includes current 2027 employer postings from Amazon, Bank of America, Boeing, Disney and ABC7; broad student pathways from Apple, Salesforce, JPMorganChase, Mastercard, Spotify, Warner Bros. Discovery, Target, Capital One, BCG, Intel, Tesla, Delta, UPS, FedEx, Walmart, Nike, Marriott, Hilton, Caterpillar, NPR, EPA, and the State Department; 2027 cohorts or announced cycles from Cold Spring Harbor, Mayo Clinic, St. Jude, The Met, AMNH, Fred Hutch, and the Library of Congress; and carefully labeled past-cycle guides that do not pretend to be open. Broad undergraduate eligibility is a starting point, not proof that every site, project, or opening accepts every class year. Program pages display their actual source-review date. The directory does not automatically refresh employer vacancies.

Sixteen field hubs include consulting, aerospace, life sciences, logistics, consumer and hospitality, manufacturing and energy, healthcare/biomedical research, museums/culture, media/communications, and government/public service. Four substantive collections cover paid college pathways, community-college routes, undergraduate summer research, and Summer 2027 programs. Twelve original guides include research statements, recommendation requests, cover letters, portfolios, and offer decisions. The guide library supports keyword and application-stage filtering; five playbooks include copyable outlines with a manual-copy fallback. Related guides, program examples, visible questions/answers, collection links, and mobile section navigation connect discovery to preparation. Collection and guide-list structured data reflects their visible inventory; no FAQ-rich-result or ranking promise is made.

Visitors can search/filter, sort by earliest college year, next future cutoff, or employer, follow year and field hubs, save programs, use preparation checklists, maintain local planning stages/notes/dates, and export CSV. Filter URLs include the sort order; resetting clears their parameters. Mobile filters collapse to preserve room for results.

The saved-application workspace has a progress overview, local search across program names and private notes, stage filters, personal-action-date sorting, and a past/today/next-seven-days view. Filters never mutate records or change employer eligibility. JSON backups preserve all saved notes/stages/dates/checkmarks; CSV exports all saved records (including checked prompt text), not just the filtered view. Backup files are read locally, limited to 256 KB, strictly validated, and previewed before restoring. Add-missing mode keeps current saved records untouched; full replacement uses a separate on-page modal with a required acknowledgement and can remove programs absent from the file. Clear uses the same safeguard. Do not substitute native confirms, which the in-app browser can auto-accept. Unknown program IDs and changed prompts are counted in the preview. Backups are not encrypted and are not automatic cloud synchronization. Unreadable local storage is not overwritten on mount.

Personal-action calendar downloads include today's and future all-day dates for non-Closed saved programs, exclude private notes and past action dates, and carry no alarms. They are distinct from the published-cutoff calendar and the temporary preparation builder; downloaded files do not update automatically. The workspace is deliberately noindex because private personal views are not search landing pages.

The `/internship-deadlines` hub lists selected sourced cutoffs and separates passed cycles from undated/role-specific pathways. Calendar (.ics) downloads contain only future published cutoffs, preserve UTC instants, and do not invent reminders or subscribe to live updates. Dates are manually reviewed; the browser clock only determines whether an existing published cutoff has passed.

The `/application-timeline` builder creates a personal six-week preparation scaffold, compresses shorter windows, optionally includes reference requests, and exports a text checklist or all-day personal calendar. It suggests a target three days before a future published cutoff when feasible and rejects targets after that source calendar date. Date entry is read from the displayed native field on submission and kept in sync across input/change/blur events. Program links preselect a public program ID; private target dates and checkmarks are not included in URLs or persisted. Same-day targets and unconfirmed/past cycles get explicit warnings. Calendar checkpoints have stable IDs, UTF-8 folding, and no alarms; they never replace the employer's exact cutoff or letter deadlines. The tool's original editorial instructions render without JavaScript; interaction needs it.

The `/compare` tool compares up to three programs, with eligibility, compensation, location, dates, preparation prompts, and official source links. Choices use tab-session storage and can be shared in a program-ID-only URL. Private planner data is never included. Comparison is noindex and excluded from the sitemap to prevent query-selection pages becoming duplicate search targets.

The contact form opens an email draft; it does not claim to send or collect a submission. No fake subscription/account flow is offered. The visual refresh uses code-native typography, layout, and SVG decoration, with no external employer logos or implied endorsements.

## AdSense activation gates

Actual revenue is not active and cannot be promised. The owner must supply and approve the account configuration; no AdSense account has been created or linked by this work.

1. Obtain AdSense account/site approval. Follow the account's verification instructions.
2. Configure the appropriate consent messages and a Google-certified CMP where required. AdSense Privacy & messaging is one supported route; confirm regional requirements for the site's audience. A custom generic cookie banner or this project's environment flag is not a substitute.
3. Create the desired display units in that account. Add `VITE_ADSENSE_CLIENT`, `VITE_ADSENSE_SLOT_TOP`, `VITE_ADSENSE_SLOT_IN_FEED`, and `VITE_ADSENSE_SLOT_SIDEBAR` in the deployment environment.
4. Only after policy/consent validation, set `VITE_ADSENSE_ENABLED=true` and rebuild. A valid configured publisher ID generates `/ads.txt`. Confirm it matches the approved account.
5. Verify units and consent messages on the deployed domain in applicable regions. Test that blocked or rejected ads do not break browsing or the planner. Do not click your own ads, encourage clicks, disguise ads as apply buttons, or place units over core controls.
6. Keep ad density modest. Review real page performance and search traffic before expanding placements. RPM, fill rate, approval, and traffic determine revenue; there is no implemented or promised ‘money printer.’

Official references: [AdSense policies](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/48182?hl=en), [Google CMP requirements](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/13554020?hl=en), [people-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content), [Search spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies), and [JobPosting requirements](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/job-posting).

## Editorial growth workflow

1. Choose an actual student question or verified program, not a keyword permutation. Research official current-cycle sources.
2. Add a record in `src/content.js` or the relevant content-expansion module with sources, reviewed date, precise accepted-year conditions, no invented compensation/remote/deadline fields, and distinct application preparation advice. New expanded program records include their own unique SEO title/description. Add guides/collections with original advice and validate all referenced program IDs and guide slugs.
3. Keep unknown rules unknown. Do not turn ‘usually third year’ into a strict minimum. Confirm seniors' participation/student-status requirements.
4. Rebuild/test. New routes, links, metadata, and sitemap entries follow the shared content automatically.
5. Check each recruiting cycle and time-sensitive deadline manually. Update the actual review date only when reverified. Contact corrections should include an official publisher link.

Retired static marketing pages are not shipped; historical versions can be recovered from repository history. Explicit aliases consolidate retired URLs into maintained destinations. The old email-queue cron is removed; legacy backend files are not used by the directory and should be separately audited before any future reuse.
