How to Apply for an Internship: The Complete Process

Updated July 1, 2026

To apply for an internship, do both: apply to posted roles, and email companies directly (which reaches the many internships that are never posted). Direct outreach — a short, specific email with your resume — consistently gets more replies than online applications alone.

The full process

  1. Build a list of 20–50 target companies.
  2. Apply to any posted roles that fit.
  3. Email the rest directly with a short, personal note.
  4. Track who you contacted and follow up once.

Why direct outreach beats applying alone

Every posted internship is crowded; the unposted ones are wide open, and the only way in is to reach out. Doing both maximizes your odds.
