STEM High School Internships: How to Get One

Updated July 1, 2026

To get a STEM high school internship, email research labs, engineering firms, tech companies, and hospitals directly. Mention a specific STEM interest or project of yours and ask if they'd take a high school intern. University labs and local tech companies are especially open to it.

Best places to find STEM internships as a high schooler

  • University research labs (email professors directly).
  • Local engineering and manufacturing firms.
  • Tech and software startups.
  • Hospitals and clinics (for pre-med interest).

Stand out with a project

Nothing beats showing you've already tinkered — a coding project, a science fair entry, a robotics team. Mention it in your first email.
