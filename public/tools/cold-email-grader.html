    How the cold email score works

    The grader reads your email like a recruiter would and rates five things: the hook and opening (does it get to the point, or open with filler?), personalization (one specific reason tied to that company?), length and concision (70 to 120 words wins), the ask (one clear, low-pressure question), and human tone (does it read like a person or like AI and buzzwords?). You get a score, a rating on each area, the three biggest fixes, and a rewritten version you can copy and send.
