    How the resume score works

    An AI trained to think like a recruiter reads your resume and grades five things: quantified impact (real numbers, not duties), clarity and formatting, relevance and focus, writing and action verbs, and length and structure. You get an overall score, a one-line verdict, a rating on each area, and the three changes that will move the needle most. It's built for students and first-time applicants, so the advice assumes you don't have ten years of experience yet.
